SKYBREAK

F-5E Tiger II

How long can you last? Wave 1 is one fighter, wave 2 is two, and so on, with newer jets, aces and new kinds of fighter mixed in as the waves climb. Every tenth wave brings the location's boss: down it to open the next location. After every wave, pick a field upgrade for the rest of the run, or a field repair. Credits and medals pay out for every wave you survive.

    Fly survival together over the internet. One pilot hosts and shares the four-letter code. Enemies get tougher and more numerous for every pilot, friendly fire and friendly missile locks are on, and a pilot who goes down comes back after the wave with half hull and one field upgrade lost.

    Aircraft
    Paint shop ·

    Fit guns to the hardpoints on your jet, up to its gun limit. Bays inside the airframe take cannons, pylons under the wings take gun pods. Every gun has weight: go past the factory fit and the jet turns and climbs a little slower, go lighter and it gets nimbler. Pods also add drag, and on the F-35 they give away its stealth. Every gun goes in the primary or the secondary fire group, and only one group fires at a time. Each group has its own heat gauge: swap with the mouse wheel, Tab or 1 / 2, and when one overheats you swap to the other automatically while it cools. Then pick one special.

    Hardpoint
    Special · right mouse or R
    Spec sheet ·

    Equip perks into your slots before you fly. They work on every jet and stay for the whole run, so pick the ones you value most. You start with 3 slots, open a 4th by downing your first boss and a 5th by downing the bosses of 3 locations. Spend medals to unlock perks and to raise each one from tier I to V. You earn a medal for every wave you survive and three for every boss.
