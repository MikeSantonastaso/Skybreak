Each sortie is a branching map of eight missions that ends with a boss. Down the boss to open the next location, and the next heat level at this one. Bring home credits for jets and paint, and skill points for the web.

How long can you last? Wave 1 is one fighter, wave 2 is two, and so on, with newer jets, aces and new kinds of fighter mixed in as the waves climb. After every wave pick one piece of gear or a field repair. Hull, missiles and the missile cooldown carry over between waves. Credits and skill points pay out for every wave you survive.
